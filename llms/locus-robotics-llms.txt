# Locus Robotics

> Locus Robotics is a warehouse-automation company that pairs autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) with an intelligent orchestration platform, LocusONE. Its robots (Locus Array, Locus Origin, Locus Vector) and the LocusHub business-intelligence engine help distribution and fulfillment operations raise pick rates and reduce labor. LocusONE integrates with warehouse management systems (WMS) through APIs. There is no public developer portal or published API specification; integration is delivered through the WMS/ERP partner program.
